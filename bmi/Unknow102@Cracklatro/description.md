# The Cracked Balatro (Cracklatro)

A massive and complete expansion for **Balatro** built on top of **Steamodded (SMODS)**. It brings unique mechanics, exclusive Secret rarity cards, Job Consumables & Application Packs, challenging Boss Blinds, strategic Decks, Vouchers, Tags, Custom Enhancements, and Seals.

---

### ✨ Features & Content

* **🌟 13 Secret Rarity Jokers**: Exclusive "Secret" badge with legendary multi-layer animations, summoned via the Spectral card *La Muchachada*.
* **🃏 24 Standard Jokers**: Diverse Common, Uncommon, and Rare Jokers offering fresh synergies and gameplay twists.
* **💼 10 Job Consumables & 3 Booster Packs**: Assign permanent professions (*Miner, Gardener, Banker, Surgeon, Alchemist, Butcher, Detective, Chef, Archaeologist, Jeweler*) or enhance cards with Job Applications.
* **🌌 Spectral Cards, Custom Enhancements & Seals**:
  * *Diamond Card, Investment Card, Lead Card, Jeweled Card*.
  * *Dark Green Seal* and unique game-bending Spectral effects.
* **👁️ Boss Blinds (Ante 3+) & Strategic Decks**: Custom Boss Blinds and Decks with unique perks and starting conditions.
* **🎟️ Vouchers & Tags**: New shop vouchers and tags like Discord Tag.
* **⚙️ Mod Compatibility**: Built-in integration with **JokerDisplay**.

---

### 📦 Installation & Requirements
* **Requires**: [Steamodded (SMODS)](https://github.com/balatro-mods/Steamodded)
* Fully compatible with standard Steamodded setups and Balatro Mod Manager.
